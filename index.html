Privacy Policy

Pin Publisher

Operated by luke0smith100 (Luke Smith), Ireland · Effective 24 September 2026

In short: Pin Publisher is a private tool that creates pins on its owner's own Pinterest account and reads performance analytics for those pins. It is not offered to the public, does not sell or share data, and keeps only what it needs to work.

1. About this app

Pin Publisher ("the App") is a personal application that connects to Pinterest through the official Pinterest API. It is operated by luke0smith100 and used only by its owner, Luke Smith, to publish pins to his own Pinterest account and to review how those pins perform. Other Pinterest users do not sign in to the App.

2. Information the App accesses

With the account owner's permission, granted through Pinterest's own authorisation screen, the App may access:

  • Account details: the connected account's username and basic profile information.
  • Boards: the list of boards on the account, so pins can be posted to the right one.
  • Pins: pins created by the App, including images, titles, descriptions and links.
  • Pin analytics: performance figures for the account's pins, such as impressions, saves, pin clicks and outbound clicks.
  • Access tokens: the OAuth tokens Pinterest issues so the App can act on the account.

The App does not access other users' private data, direct messages, or payment information.

3. How the information is used

  • To create and publish pins to the owner's boards.
  • To read and review analytics for the owner's pins.
  • To keep the connection to Pinterest working.

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing the owner's own Pinterest content.

4. Sharing

No data obtained through the Pinterest API is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Data may pass through the hosting or storage service the App runs on, solely to operate the App.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and are never published or shared. Analytics data may be kept for the owner's own reporting. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored data from unauthorised access.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest under Settings → Security and logins → Apps, which invalidates the App's tokens. When access is revoked, stored tokens and associated data are deleted.
